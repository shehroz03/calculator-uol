Introduction

Welcome to UOL CGPA Pro. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and website located at uolcal.broadsolutiontech.com.

By using our app or website, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and full name when you create an account.
  • Academic Data: Grades, credit hours, semester data, and CGPA calculations that you enter.
  • Device Information: Basic device and usage data for app functionality.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the CGPA calculation service
  • Save and sync your academic data across sessions
  • Authenticate your account securely
  • Improve our app and user experience

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (a secure cloud database platform) and AsyncStorage on your device. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your personal information.

Each user's data is completely isolated — no other user can access your academic data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is used solely to provide you with the UOL CGPA Pro service.

User Accounts

You can create an account using your email and password. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. You may delete your account at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

UOL CGPA Pro is designed for university students (age 18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies for basic functionality. We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising tracking.
